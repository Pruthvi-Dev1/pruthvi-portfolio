I'm Pruthvi P R, a Software Engineer at Provab Technosoft in Bengaluru, where I architect and build full-stack web applications that handle real-world complexity at scale. My foundation in Information Science Engineering from SDM Institute of Technology shaped my thinking around systems, efficiency, and elegant code.
